## Owner-managed profile review

- Confirm the verified Google Business Profile represents this address and academy name accurately.
- Review its primary category against the actual business. Choose the most accurate available category in the profile; do not claim a category option without checking its current availability.
- Confirm normal operating hours and holiday exceptions with the owner. Published class start times alone are insufficient to establish business hours.
- Keep the website destination on the live production domain, not the noindex preview. Update appointment/booking destinations only after checking the real form flow.
- Add fresh owner-approved photographs of the academy, instructors and actual classes. Obtain appropriate permissions for identifiable students.
- Request honest feedback from actual students without incentives or filtering out negative reviewers. Do not fabricate testimonials or copy competitor reviews.
- Review existing directory listings before creating duplicates. Correct wrong address/phone details rather than adding alternate business names for keywords.

## Measurement plan

No Search Console, analytics, Ahrefs or Semrush data was connected during this audit. Baselines must be collected rather than invented.

After approved launch, record monthly:

1. Google Search Console: indexed canonical pages, impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by query and page. Separate branded searches from non-branded local searches.
2. Google Business Profile: available call, direction and website-interaction metrics. Note that interactions are not confirmed enrollments.
3. Free-trial inquiries: actual provider submissions and the number that become visits. A click on “Free class” is only an intent signal.
4. Page experience: field LCP, INP and CLS when enough data exists; lab tests for diagnosing specific issues.

Proposed analytics events, pending an approved analytics/privacy setup: `booking_open`, `phone_click`, `sms_click`, `email_click`, `directions_click`, and a provider-confirmed `trial_request_complete`. Do not record names, email addresses, phone numbers or comment text in analytics events. Do not treat a button click as a successful form submission.
